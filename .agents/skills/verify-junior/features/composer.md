# Composer

The composer is the message box under the transcript of a Conversation ("Continue this conversation"). A message sent while Junior is idle shows in the transcript at once. A message sent while Junior replies waits in the queue above the box, where the user can edit, delete, or steer it. `Stop` ends the running Turn. The box keeps an unsent draft, takes files, and takes a quote of a selected reply. Typing `$` opens a menu of skills to insert, `@` a menu of people, Conversations, Tasks, and files to mention, and `#` a menu of pull requests and Slack channels. It shows only in the Conversation view, not in the event log, and only to users who may reply. A question card in the transcript is `inbox#waiting`.

## Sub-features

- `send-inline` A message sent while no Turn runs shows in the transcript at once, not in the queue, and Junior replies to it.
- `enter-sends` Enter sends. Shift+Enter adds a line and sends nothing. `Send` is disabled while the draft is empty.
- `draft-kept` Unsent text stays in the box after the user leaves the Conversation and comes back in the same browser.
- `queue` A message sent while Junior replies waits in "Pending messages" as `Queued`. Junior reads it after the current reply.
- `queue-edit` `Edit queued message` takes the message out of the queue and puts its text back in the box.
- `queue-delete` `Delete queued message` takes the message out of the queue, and Junior never reads it.
- `steer` `Steer queued message` marks the message `Steered`. Junior reads it at its next step, not after the whole reply, and answers it.
- `stop` `Stop` ends the running Turn. Queued messages leave the queue, their text goes back in the box, and the Conversation shows `Stopped`.
- `attach` `Attach files` (or a drop or paste) adds up to 10 files of up to 250 MB each. Each shows above the text with a `Remove <name>` button, and the message sends with them.
- `send-failed` A send that fails stays in the transcript with "Could not send." and `Retry`.
- `respond-quote` Select text in a reply of Junior and choose `Respond`. The box gets the text as a Markdown quote.
- `conversation-view-only` `Open event log` hides the composer. `Back to conversation` shows it again, with the draft.
- `skill-menu` Typing `$` opens the `Skills` grid of installed skills. Arrow keys move the active row, and Enter or Tab inserts `$name `.
- `skill-details` The details pane shows the name, source, and full description of the active row. It follows arrow keys at once and the pointer after it rests on a row. `View full skill` or Ctrl/Cmd+I opens the SKILL.md drawer, and closing it puts focus and the caret back in the box.
- `skill-split` The menu shows the list beside the pane when the menu is at least 32rem wide, and stacked rows when it is narrower. The 606px reply composer at 1440 with Details open stays split.
- `skill-menu-mobile` Below 32rem, `Details for $name` expands the row in place with the description and `View full skill`, and inserts nothing. A tap on the name inserts it.
- `skill-esc` Esc closes the menu and leaves the question cards in the transcript open.
- `skill-card` A card describes the skill just inserted, and the complete `$name` that the caret moves into. It sits on that `$name`: below it in the composer on `/`, above it in a reply composer. An edit or Esc hides it, and it never shows with the menu.
- `skill-token` A complete `$name` of an installed skill in the box shows in the link color. Other names and names in code stay plain. A click or a tap on it only moves the caret, so the `skill-card` shows and no drawer opens. The name stays plain text: Backspace deletes one character, Ctrl/Cmd+Z puts it back, and the message sends the exact text.
- `mention-menu` Typing `@` opens the `Mentions` listbox. Its `People` group has people in the Conversation's Slack workspace. Arrow keys move the selected row, and Enter or Tab inserts it.
- `mention-chip` An inserted person shows as the button `@<name>` in the box and sends as `<@U…>`.
- `mention-card` Hover or a tap on the chip opens the shared person card. In a public Conversation, its `Invite` adds a User to the Conversation's feed and then shows `Subscribed`. A person who is not a User gets no `Invite`.
- `mention-sources` Below `People`, the `Conversations` group has the viewer's Conversations, `Tasks` has this Conversation's Tasks and Threads, and `Files` has its files.
- `hash-menu` Typing `#` opens the `References` listbox, with `Pull requests` that Junior tracks and the Slack `Channels` it worked in. `a#b`, `# heading`, and a link with `#` stay text.
- `reference-chip` An inserted Conversation or Task sends as a labeled link, a pull request (`repo#123`) as `<url|owner/repo#123>`, a channel as `<#C…|name>`, and a file as its name. After the send, each Conversation, Task, and pull request is linked work once.
- `read-only` A user who may not reply to a private Conversation sees "Only participants can reply to a private conversation." in place of the box.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Dashboard: open any Conversation (`/conversations/<id>`) in the Conversation view. A new Conversation from the composer on `/` opens there after its first send.
- The queue ("Pending messages") shows above the box while messages wait. Its row buttons have the names above.
- `Stop` shows beside `Send` while a Turn runs.
- `Respond` shows beside `Quick question` when the user selects text in a reply.
- The skill menu opens on `$` in any composer, on `/` or in a Conversation. Rows are `gridcell`s named `$<skill>`. The drawer is the dialog `$<skill>`, closed by `Close $<skill>`.
- The `@` and `#` menus open in any composer. Rows are `option`s under a `group` for each source. A person row is named after the person. The card is the dialog `<name>`. `Invite` shows only in a public, unarchived Conversation.
- `Conversation menu`, then `Open event log` or `Back to conversation`.
- The new dashboard (Settings, "Try the new dashboard") uses the same composer.

## Driving it with browse.mjs

Preconditions:

- `instance.mjs launch` (no `--mock`) started a new instance, and `instance.mjs doctor` passes. `read-only` needs a `--mock` instance instead.
- `E=$RUN_DIR/evidence/composer`, `C='role=textbox[name="Continue this conversation"]'`, `P='[aria-label="Pending messages"]'`.
- For the skill menu: `H='role=textbox[name="Start a conversation"]'`, `K='role=grid[name="Skills"]'`, `V='role=button[name="View full skill"]'`, `G='role=group[name="$example-local"]'` (the card).
- `R='article:has-text("junior-example")'` matches a reply from Junior.
- The box is a rich-text editor. Its text shows in the ARIA snapshot as `paragraph:` lines under `textbox "Continue this conversation"`, and a mention as `button "@<name>"` in the paragraph. Read it with `grep -A3 'textbox "Continue' NAME.aria.yml`.

- **Send, Enter, quote, draft, view (`send-inline`, `enter-sends`, `respond-quote`, `draft-kept`, `conversation-view-only`).** Run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/basics --timeout 300000 --goto / --fill 'role=textbox[name="Start a conversation"]=Reply with exactly: pong' --press Enter --expect "$C" --idle --expect 'role=button[name="Send"][disabled]' --fill "$C=Reply with exactly: the build passed on every runner today" --press Shift+Enter --expect 'role=button[name="Send"][disabled=false]' --snap shift-enter --press Enter --expect 'article:has-text("Reply with exactly: the build passed")' --absent "$P >> text=the build passed" --snap sent --idle --expect "$R >> text=\"the build passed on every runner today\"" --select "$R >> p >> text=\"the build passed on every runner today\"" --click 'role=button[name="Respond"]' --snap quoted --goto / --expect 'role=textbox[name="Start a conversation"]' --back quoted --expect "$C" --snap back --click 'role=button[name="Conversation menu"]' --click 'role=menuitem[name="Open event log"]' --absent "$C" --snap event-log --click 'role=button[name="Conversation menu"]' --click 'role=menuitem[name="Back to conversation"]' --expect "$C" --snap conversation`. After Shift+Enter, `Send` is still enabled, so nothing was sent. The message shows in the transcript, not in the queue, and Junior replies. `quoted`, `back`, and `conversation` show `> the build passed on every runner today` in the box. `event-log` has no box.
- **Queue, delete, steer (`queue`, `queue-delete`, `steer`).** Run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/queue --timeout 240000 --goto / --fill 'role=textbox[name="Start a conversation"]=Write the numbers from 1 to 600 in words, one per line, and end with the line DONE.' --press Enter --expect 'role=button[name="Stop"]' --expect 'text=is thinking…' --fill "$C=Also reply with the word QUEUEDONE." --press Enter --expect "$P >> text=QUEUEDONE" --expect "$P >> [aria-label=\"Queued\"]" --snap queued --click "$P >> role=button[name=\"Delete queued message\"]" --absent "$P >> text=QUEUEDONE" --snap deleted --fill "$C=After you finish, add the line STEERED." --press Enter --click "$P >> role=button[name=\"Steer queued message\"]" --expect "$P >> [aria-label=\"Steered\"]" --snap steered --idle --expect "$R >> text=STEERED" --absent "$R >> text=QUEUEDONE" --snap reply`. The message waits as `Queued`. After the delete, Junior never says `QUEUEDONE`. The steered message shows `Steered`, and Junior answers it with `STEERED`.
- **Edit and stop (`queue-edit`, `stop`).** Run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/edit-stop --timeout 240000 --goto / --fill 'role=textbox[name="Start a conversation"]=Write the numbers from 1 to 600 in words, one per line, and end with the line DONE.' --press Enter --expect 'role=button[name="Stop"]' --expect 'text=is thinking…' --fill "$C=Also reply with the word QUEUEDONE." --press Enter --expect "$P >> [aria-label=\"Queued\"]" --click "$P >> role=button[name=\"Edit queued message\"]" --expect 'role=button[name="Send"][disabled=false]' --absent "$P >> text=QUEUEDONE" --snap edited --press Enter --expect "$P >> text=QUEUEDONE" --snap requeued --click 'role=button[name="Stop"]' --absent 'role=button[name="Stop"]' --absent "$P >> text=QUEUEDONE" --expect 'role=button[name="Send"][disabled=false]' --snap stopped --idle --absent "$R >> text=QUEUEDONE" --snap after`. `edited` and `stopped` show `Also reply with the word QUEUEDONE.` in the box. In `after`, the Conversation link in the list ends with `Stopped`.
- **Files (`attach`).** Run `mkdir -p $E; printf 'The release codename is marmalade.\n' > $E/codename.txt; node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/attach --timeout 300000 --goto / --fill 'role=textbox[name="Start a conversation"]=Reply with exactly: ready' --press Enter --expect "$C" --idle --upload "role=button[name=\"Choose files\"]=$E/codename.txt" --expect 'role=button[name="Remove codename.txt"]' --snap attached --fill "$C=What is the release codename in the attached file? Reply with only the codename." --press Enter --absent 'role=button[name="Remove codename.txt"]' --idle --expect "$R >> text=/marmalade/i" --snap reply`. The file shows with `Remove codename.txt` before the send, and Junior answers `marmalade`.
- **Failed send (`send-failed`).** After the `attach` drive, while local uploads fail (see `Gotchas`), run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/send-failed --timeout 60000 --goto "$(cat $E/attach/attached.url.txt)" --upload "role=button[name=\"Choose files\"]=$E/codename.txt" --expect 'role=button[name="Remove codename.txt"]' --fill "$C=What is the release codename in the attached file? Reply with only the codename." --press Enter --expect 'text=Could not send.' --expect 'role=button[name="Retry"]' --snap failed`. The message stays in the transcript with "Could not send." and `Retry`.
- **Skill menu and details (`skill-menu`, `skill-details`).** On a `--mock` instance, run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/skill-menu --goto / --type "$H=Run \$exa" --expect "$K" --expect 'text=From the example-bundle plugin' --snap menu --press ArrowDown --expect 'text=App or local skill' --snap pane-arrow --hover "$K >> role=gridcell[name=/example-bundle-help/]" --expect 'text=From the example-bundle plugin' --snap pane-hover --click "$V" --expect 'role=dialog[name="$example-bundle-help"]' --snap drawer --click 'role=button[name="Close $example-bundle-help"]' --absent 'role=dialog' --press Control+i --expect 'role=dialog[name="$example-bundle-help"]' --click 'role=button[name="Close $example-bundle-help"]' --absent 'role=dialog' --press Enter --absent "$K" --snap inserted`. The pane follows ArrowDown to `$example-local` and the pointer back to `$example-bundle-help`. Ctrl+I after the close works only because focus is back in the box. `inserted` shows `paragraph: Run $example-bundle-help`.
- **Split width and Esc (`skill-split`, `skill-esc`).** On a `--mock` instance, run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/skill-esc --goto '/conversations/internal%3Aquestions-qa' --expect 'role=complementary[name="Conversation details"]' --expect 'role=region[name=/^Asked /] >> role=region[name="Question"]' --type "$C=\$" --expect "$K" --expect "$V" --absent 'role=button[name=/^Details for/]' --snap split --press Escape --absent "$K" --expect 'role=region[name=/^Asked /] >> role=region[name="Question"]' --snap esc`. At the default width 1440, Details is open and the menu is 606px wide. The pane shows and no row has a chevron, so it is split. After Esc the menu is gone and the question cards stay.
- **Mobile rows (`skill-menu-mobile`).** On a `--mock` instance, run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/skill-menu-mobile --touch --width 390 --goto / --type "$H=\$exa" --expect "$K" --absent "$V" --snap rows --tap 'role=button[name="Details for $example-local"]' --expect 'role=button[name="Details for $example-local"][expanded]' --expect "$V" --snap expanded --tap "$V" --expect 'role=dialog[name="$example-local"]' --tap 'role=button[name="Close $example-local"]' --absent 'role=dialog' --tap "$K >> role=gridcell[name=/example-local/]" --absent "$K" --snap inserted`. `expanded` still shows `paragraph: $exa`, and `inserted` shows `paragraph: $example-local`.
- **Skill card (`skill-card`).** On a `--mock` instance, run `node $S/browse.mjs --out $E/skill-card --goto / --type "$H=Run \$exa" --expect "$K" --press ArrowDown --press Enter --absent "$K" --expect "$G" --snap inserted --type "$H=now" --absent "$G" --press ArrowLeft --press ArrowLeft --press ArrowLeft --press ArrowLeft --expect "$G" --absent "$K" --snap caret --click "$G >> $V" --expect 'role=dialog[name="$example-local"]' --click 'role=button[name="Close $example-local"]' --absent role=dialog --press Escape --absent "$G" --snap esc --press End --press ArrowLeft --press ArrowLeft --press ArrowLeft --press ArrowLeft --expect "$G" --press Backspace --expect "$K" --absent "$G" --snap edit`. ## Gotchas

- A new Conversation first shows a pending view with its own disabled box, also named "Continue this conversation". Enter there sends nothing, and the typed text moves to the real box when the page swaps. Wait for `--idle` or for `role=button[name="Stop"]` before you fill `$C`.
- A message sent before the transcript shows `is thinking…` can go to the transcript instead of the queue, and Junior reads it after the reply. Wait for `text=is thinking…` on the Conversation page before a queue step.
- A steer cannot cut into one long model call. With the 600-number prompt, `STEERED` shows as its own message after the numbers. The proof is the `Steered` row and the answer, not where the answer shows.
- The queue steps must finish before the reply does. The 600-number prompt gives about a minute. A faster model can need a longer prompt.
- Edit restores the text only after the server confirms the delete. Wait for `Send` to turn enabled before you snap or check the box.
- `Respond` shows only for a selection of at least a few words. A one-word reply, such as `pong`, shows no menu.
- Drafts live in browser storage, and each `browse.mjs` run is a new browser. Leave and come back inside one run.
- `Choose files` is a hidden file input that Playwright exposes as a button. Use it with `--upload`. `Attach files` opens the system file picker, which a headless browser cannot use.
- Local attachment storage is the compose `s3` service (`localhost:7072`). The browser uploads to it from `localhost:3000`, so the bucket needs CORS. The compose service has no CORS today, so the upload fails, the send shows "Could not send.", and `attach` cannot be proven past the file row locally. A failed send lives only in that browser tab. A new `browse.mjs` run does not show it.
- The skill drives need only the skill list and no Turn, so they use `--mock`. Its "Plan the billing migration" Conversation (`internal:questions-qa`) is the only local path to question cards in a transcript. The skills come from the example app: `example-local` (app or local) and `example-bundle-help` (plugin `example-bundle`).
- Use `--type`, not `--fill`, before Esc. A `--fill` value moves the caret without the key events a person makes, and the next key can reopen the menu, so Esc looks broken.
- Hover changes the pane only after the pointer rests about 120ms on a row. `--hover` leaves the pointer there, so `--expect` waits it out.
- The mobile rows need `--touch --width 390`. A width alone keeps a mouse, and `--tap` needs the touch screen.
- On `/`, the box is the Lead's, named "Continue this conversation". The `skill-token` drives use `$C` there.
- The `skill-token` click needs the box to hold only `$example-local`. `--click` and `--tap` hit the middle of the text, and the reference is text, not an element. `--press Home` first moves the caret out of the reference, so the Esc before it no longer keeps the card closed.
- After Esc, the menu and card stay closed until the caret leaves that reference. Move the caret out, as `--press End` does, before a step needs them again.
- Wait for `--absent role=dialog` after you close the drawer. The box gets focus back only then, and an earlier key goes to the page.
- The mention and reference drives use `--mock`, because local development has one User. Wait for the menu to stop being busy before Enter: a row below a source that still loads does not insert. Its people search and `Invite` are mock routes, so they prove only what the dashboard shows. The mock public Conversation `slack:CQA123:1770000000.000100` shows `Invite`.
- Run the Riley Park steps in their own `browse.mjs` run. After `Invite` in the same run, a later `--type "$C=@rile"` and `--press Tab` left no `@Riley Park` chip.
- `read-only` needs a second user, which local development does not have (the viewer is always `dev@example.com`). The mock fixture is the only local path, and it proves only what the dashboard shows.
