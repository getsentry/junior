## Long-term Knowledge

### Architecture

<!-- lore:01a10d6a-ebca-73d5-8b9b-0ebc568c568a -->

- **distillation_batch durable recovery**: Chose persisted \`distillation_batch\` and \`distillation_batch_done\` events plus delayed checks over awaiting a Batch result inside the ten-minute worker lock because Gateway batches may take up to 24 hours. Submit with a stable idempotency key, persist the Gateway job reference, and match results by unique request ID rather than arrival order. Reject duplicate, extra, missing, or non-text results; failed or stale batches leave source history raw. Record known billed costs separately from marked-estimated costs, including when stale history prevents observation writes; purged Conversations reject late writes.

<!-- lore:01a10279-c5d4-730d-a237-1c153b4c57de -->

- **Junior eval results reporting API**: Chose Junior’s Conversation reporting API over stored-row or runtime-object reads because eval assertions must match the user-visible view without exposing private content. \`conversation.compactions\` reports history replacements directly and is suitable for a first-Turn zero-compaction assertion; use \`readDistillationUsage()\` to distinguish later capacity compactions from distillation replacements. Mainline fixture changes removed several former \`currentUsage()\` diagnostics, so assuming older metadata fields still exist looks convenient but can make assertions invalid; inspect the current fixture contract. Keep assistant and auxiliary costs separate, since Gateway observations may overlap assistant calls; missing cache counters mean unknown, not zero.

<!-- lore:01a1019b-f29e-75ad-a260-6023b18957d7 -->

- **Junior history replacement events**: Chose durable \`handoff\` and \`compaction\` history-replacement events over silently mutating Pi state because replay and crash recovery must know which active history and resume boundary were committed. Conversation events remain append-only; a replacement stores its history and updates Pi messages and the checkpoint together. A model-only switch is not a \`handoff\`: that event means history replacement, and old events must remain readable. Subsequent compaction must preserve the switched profile.

<!-- lore:01a103ed-576d-713a-b744-1549d229c213 -->

- **Junior Turn distillation lifecycle**: Chose a default-off post-\`turn_completed\` observation worker and a priced replacement before a later Turn’s first model request over reply-path distillation or in-turn rewriting because observation calls must not delay replies and active work needs exact history. \`JUNIOR_CONTEXT_DISTILLATION_ENABLED\` gates worker registration and use; the worker appends Conversation-scoped evidence, not Pi messages, and adoption commits a durable \`compaction\` replacement and resume checkpoint. Ordinary capacity compaction remains the fallback. Luna was the original observation model, not a permanent architectural choice; Burak Yigit Kaya directed migration to Jev \[\[01a112ee-3b56-708c-a9df-bf328ff2cfca]]. Do not infer that migration is complete or activate production distillation \[\[01a11199-0afe-7a3e-9951-d81229160d81]].

<!-- lore:01a1019b-f4c0-7a60-9ac8-5eed1dc8c467 -->

- **Lore offline compaction assembly**: Chose offline assembly from stored distillations over a dedicated \`lore-compact\` LLM call because existing observations can form the summary without another synthesis call. \`generateCompactionSummary()\` urgently distills outstanding messages when needed, then assembles distillations and a bounded undistilled raw tail so a distillation failure does not erase recent work. The \`COMPACT_SUMMARY_TEMPLATE\` and \`buildCompactPrompt()\` in \`prompt.ts\` are legacy dead code, not the active compaction path.

### Decision

<!-- lore:01a1020a-e234-7db1-bdb1-643d5da8c937 -->

- **hexPlugin waitForHexThread**: Chose a runtime \`hexPlugin()\` with wrapped \`get_thread\` over the former manifest-only Hex plugin because model-driven polling incurred a serial model call for each wait. Expose \`create_thread\` and \`continue_thread\`, but let \`waitForHexThread\` make at most five bounded polls per invocation; the skill permits two invocations, ten polls total. Keep \`unknown\` and partial responses distinct from completion, and propagate authorization pauses rather than treating them as results.

<!-- lore:01a1020a-e0ff-7f5d-a537-a2ddb3dcff87 -->

- **shouldReuseHandoffHistory economics**: Chose priced reuse of safe, fully committed Junior history over automatically summarizing every in-turn model switch because a summary adds a model call and cold replacement write. \`shouldReuseHandoffHistory()\` compares summary, cold-write, and cached-read costs; reuse also requires target-model context fit, instruction provenance, and valid tool pairs. If prior work is not fully committed, use a summary—not a raw replacement. A close cost call keeps exact history; materially cheaper or unsafe reuse takes the summary path.

### Gotcha

<!-- lore:01a10d6a-eaa9-7e89-8c2d-b0ff1056c339 -->

Fix: retain SDK 6 for the shared runtime and isolate the SDK 7/Gateway 4 batch client; verify the package graph and typechecks after dependency changes. The Gateway Batch API must not drive an unrelated shared-runtime upgrade.

<!-- lore:01a10ae1-00e1-7c11-b216-5bba9f0034da -->

- **compactWithDistillations replacement gates**: Trap: observations look sufficient to replace Junior history, but \`compactWithDistillations()\` also requires an exact projected-history match and rejects replacements at or above the model input limit. Fix: diagnose those gates separately from worker activation; below the capacity trigger, an unpriced replacement is rejected because a shorter history alone does not establish savings. A successful distillation replacement records its covered sequence, token estimates, expected calls, and pricing status.

<!-- lore:01a103ed-5938-7546-9495-d3ede9b29e51 -->

- **dashboard compaction reason union**: Trap: typing every \`compaction.details\` as \`reason: "capacity"\` looks right because capacity was the only prior replacement path, but distillation adds different detail fields. Fix: carry the capacity/distillation union through the Conversation API, dashboard context-event type, and Markdown export; read trigger, input limit, and message-count fields only for capacity compactions.

<!-- lore:01a11238-f95b-787e-adc4-55b43d412cb0 -->

- **Dashboard mock reset bundled app**: Trap: resetting Dashboard mock preferences through an unbundled module looks equivalent to resetting the E2E app, but tsup inlines mock fixtures into \`dist/app.js\`, so the import can affect a different copy. Fix: call \`resetMockDashboardState()\` from the built Dashboard app to clear its process-local state. Per-User distillation controls were removed; do not restore their mock state merely to preserve this old test path.

<!-- lore:01a103ed-58cc-7714-939b-2ac9818a8106 -->

- **distillation model-visible tokens**: Trap: summing historical assistant usage counters looks like a reasonable estimate of raw-history size, but those counters describe earlier requests, not text sent in the next request; they can make a shorter replacement appear no smaller. Fix: estimate serialized model-visible role and content for both raw and replacement history before applying the cost gate.

<!-- lore:01a10d6a-ed13-729b-b4e8-7d2306a70bd3 -->

- **Gateway Batch request contract**: Trap: provider-native IDs, arrival order, or default reasoning look usable because the provider executes the Batch, but Gateway owns lookup, results reorder, and default reasoning may add cost. Fix: persist the serializable Gateway \`BatchReference\`, route status/results through Gateway, match by request ID, and set reasoning explicitly for the selected observation model; the earlier Luna-specific \`reasoning: "none"\` must be rechecked when migrating to Jev. Resolve credentials per operation, use a stable idempotency key, obtain successful-result cost from Gateway generation info, mark the 404 fallback estimated, and never discount merely eligible work.

<!-- lore:01a102ee-9b93-7298-ac47-c8c0c8917b1c -->

- **gatewayModelCalls double-counting**: Chose getsentry/junior’s authorized Conversation reporting API over direct stored-row reads because evals must measure the user-visible view without leaking private payloads. \`readModelCalls()\` reads numeric assistant-message usage; \`readModelTotals()\` groups it by model. Keep auxiliary costs and gateway observations separate: they can overlap assistant calls. Conversation model-call reports allowlist provider, API, stop reason, and summarized usage; never expose opaque provider metadata.

<!-- lore:01a1130a-4320-7749-be62-80c41acc1070 -->

- **gh run download lacks --attempt**: Trap: downloading an artifact by run ID and name looks sufficient when rerun attempts produce same-name artifacts, but \`gh run download\` has no \`--attempt\` flag and can leave the attempt ambiguous. Fix: list artifact IDs and creation times first, then select the artifact from the intended attempt before interpreting its results.

<!-- lore:01a1030d-b03e-7d13-928d-d2e4a76c887f -->

- **gh run rerun --job databaseId**: Trap: the job number in a GitHub Actions browser URL looks like the identifier for \`gh run rerun --job\`, but the command requires the job’s \`databaseId\`. Fix: obtain it from \`gh run view \<run-id> --json jobs --jq '.jobs\[] | {name, databaseId}'\` and rerun the intended shard. \`gh run rerun --failed\` can also rerun failed dependencies, so it is not an exact-shard substitute.

<!-- lore:01a1019b-f456-73d9-a39b-886487528108 -->

- **gradient.ts currentTurnStart protection**: Trap: ordinary Lore windowing looks suitable for a long serial tool chain because it can strip old tool output, but \`currentTurnStart()\` protects the entire active turn and its tool pairs; \`tryFit()\` reserves that turn against the raw budget. A turn that alone exceeds the budget cannot fit normally. Fix: checkpoint completed in-turn work at a safe boundary while preserving the current instruction, open plan, provenance, recent results, and valid tool-call/result pairs. Tracked in BYK/loreai#1971.

<!-- lore:01a1020a-e4c4-779a-8382-284908f77107 -->

- **gradient.ts Layer-0 cost target**: Trap: Lore’s default \`$0.10\` cache-read target looks like a safe token cap because \`computeLayer0Cap()\` uses the routed model’s cache price, but at \`$0.20\` per million it yields 500k tokens, above Junior’s 400k host limit. The one-call compression comparison also omits summary work, uncached tail writes, and later calls. Fix: calibrate against capacity and measured multi-call costs instead of copying the fixed target into Junior. Tracked in BYK/loreai#1970.

<!-- lore:01a1019b-f530-7a0e-8414-b2ffd40b80de -->

- **gradient.ts per-request ModelBudget**: Trap: setting module-global model pricing and caps before an asynchronous transform looks convenient, but concurrent requests can then inherit another model’s limits. Fix: pass \`ModelBudget\` to \`transform()\` for synchronous application to that request; reset unavailable cache prices and Layer-0 caps rather than carrying prior values. Use the actual routed provider’s model specification.

<!-- lore:01a1029c-d121-7a46-8117-943dca263546 -->

One matched-route integration comparison found higher candidate cost overall and in handoff cases, so it established no savings. A cancelled shard makes available-case totals unsuitable as full-suite totals.

### Pattern

<!-- lore:01a10303-21ea-732b-b9ee-a782f245a945 -->

- **Junior context evaluation ladder**: Steps: 1. Run repeated off/on full-runtime Turns at the same SHA and model route — loaded history alone never starts the observer. 2. Count every baseline \`compaction\` reason — zero distillation replacements does not prove ordinary capacity compaction was absent. 3. Confirm observations and a \`priced: true\` replacement; compare completed-case quality, focused-test results, later-history recall, latency, assistant plus auxiliary cost, cache counters, and repeated work. 4. Inspect the synthetic Gateway Batch probe separately — submission or estimated cost does not establish billed savings. Gotchas: - A shorter replacement, lower assistant cost, or a reference appearing in the final reply looks like success, but failed case assertions and higher auxiliary cost can reverse the conclusion. Missing counters mean unknown. Verify: - \[ ] Both arms completed and passed on matching routes; baseline capacity-compaction count is known. - \[ ] Enabled arm used a priced replacement without losing reply quality or increasing completed-task cost. - \[ ] Batch state and estimated versus billed cost are labeled.
